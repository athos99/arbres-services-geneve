Our Specialized Services

Each tree has its own biology, architecture and history. We adapt our intervention techniques with the utmost respect for the tree and surrounding infrastructure.

Tree maintenance and pruning by climbing or crane

Modern pruning does not consist of "mutilating" crowns, but of accompanying the tree in its development in inhabited areas. Our arborist climbers access the entire crown on rope with lightness or use an elevating crane depending on site constraints.

  • Reasoned & health pruning: removal of dead wood, dying or rubbing branches.
  • Thinning & lightening: reducing wind catch and allowing light without unbalancing the tree.
  • Cohabitation pruning: clearing facades, roofs, power lines and property boundaries.
  • Dynamic cabling: preventive securing of weakened main branches without wounding drills.

Fruit tree and ornamental shrub pruning

A well-cared fruit tree offers a generous harvest and naturally resists cryptogamic diseases and parasites. With our knowledge of plant biology, we practice pruning respectful of vegetative cycles.

  • Fruiting pruning: apple trees, pear trees, cherry trees, plum trees and fig trees.
  • Old orchard restoration: restoring vigor and balance to untended trees.
  • Shrub & living hedge pruning: valorizing indigenous meadow hedges and respecting nesting periods.
  • Irrigation & soil amendment advice: natural soil care for a thriving root system.
Consult for your fruit trees

Special felling and stump grinding

When a tree shows irreversible decline or directly threatens buildings or people, felling becomes essential. We master high-precision felling techniques in very confined spaces.

  • Retention dismantling: every branch and trunk section is brake and guided to the ground by rope with zero impact.
  • Crane felling: ideal when the trunk is hollow or too dangerous to climb.
  • Mechanical stump grinding: we grind the stump up to 30-40 cm below ground level without disturbing the garden.
  • Administrative procedures: advice on felling permits with the cantons of Geneva and Vaud.
