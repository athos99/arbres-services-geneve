Expertise & Safety

Specialized Tree Services

Every tree has its individual biological architecture. We tailor our climbing and mechanical operations to preserve natural vitality and ensure absolute safety.

Service 01

Tree care & pruning by rope climbing or aerial cherry picker

Modern arboriculture respects the tree's natural healing collar. Our certified tree climbers move smoothly throughout the canopy on ropes, or operate via cherry picker platforms for inaccessible sites.

  • Crown thinning & deadwooding: removing hazardous dead branches and promoting airflow.
  • Structural crown balance: lightening heavy branches without stressing the tree.
  • Clearance pruning: keeping branches away from facades, roofs, and power lines.
  • Dynamic crown cabling: non-invasive bracing for split-prone major limbs.
Request diagnostic visit
Service 02

Fruit tree pruning and ornamental shrubs

Proper fruit tree pruning ensures abundant harvests while protecting plants against fungal diseases. We apply gentle pruning timed to each species' physiological calendar.

  • Fruit-bearing pruning: apple, pear, cherry, plum, fig trees.
  • Orchard rejuvenation: restoring vigor and shape to neglected heritage trees.
  • Native hedges & shrubs: supporting local pollinators and respecting bird nesting periods.
  • Soil and root care: organic mulching and natural amendments.
Consult for your fruit trees
Service 03

Delicate tree felling & stump grinding

When a tree is severely decayed or threatens buildings, precision sectional felling is necessary. We dismantle trees piece by piece with controlled rope rigging.

  • Sectional dismantling with rigging: branches and trunk logs lowered gently without touching the ground.
  • Cherry picker platform felling: optimal when trunks are hollow or unsafe to climb.
  • Mechanical stump grinding: removes the stump 30-40 cm below soil surface without digging up the lawn.
  • Administrative guidance: assistance with felling permit procedures in Geneva and Vaud cantons.

Natural landscaping & garden woodwork

Born into a florist family and trained in landscape arboriculture, Bendik Häuserman crafts outdoor environments combining natural beauty and biodiversity.

  • Terrace landscaping: blending natural wood, mineral paving and lush greenery.
  • Living hedgerows: planting native honey-rich species for privacy and wildlife.
  • Garden woodwork: pergolas, screens, planter boxes and wooden borders.
  • Tree planting: choosing resilient species suited to local soil and future climate.

Service 05

Transport up to 4 tonnes & snow removal

Reliable logistical equipment for our worksites and seasonal services for condominiums, businesses, and private residences in Geneva and Vaud.

  • 4T transport: rapid evacuation of timber logs, branches, and topsoil.
