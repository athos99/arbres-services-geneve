Our Specialist Services

Every tree has its own biology, architecture and history. We adapt our intervention techniques with the utmost respect for the tree and surrounding infrastructure.

Tree maintenance and pruning by climbing or bucket truck

Modern pruning is not about "mutilating" crowns, but about accompanying the tree in its development within built environments. Our climbing arborists access the entire crown via rope with lightness, or we use an elevating bucket truck depending on site constraints.

  • Reasoned & hygienic pruning: removal of dead wood, declining or rubbing branches.
  • Thinning & lightening: reducing wind resistance and introducing light without unbalancing the tree.
  • Cohabitation pruning: clearing facades, roofs, aerial cables and boundary lines.
  • Dynamic cabling: preventive securing of weakened main branches without damaging drilling.

Fruit tree and ornamental shrub pruning

A well-cared fruit tree yields abundant harvests and resists cryptogamic diseases and parasites naturally. With our knowledge of plants, we practice pruning that respects vegetative cycles.

  • Fruit-bearing pruning: apple trees, pear trees, cherry trees, plum trees and fig trees.
  • Restoration of old orchards: restoring vigor and balance to trees left unmaintained.
  • Shrub pruning & hedgerows: enhancing indigenous agricultural hedgerows and respecting bird nesting periods.
  • Irrigation and amendment advice: natural soil care for a thriving root system.

Special felling and stump grinding

When a tree shows irreversible decline or directly threatens buildings or people, its felling becomes essential. We master high-precision felling techniques in very confined spaces.

  • Teardown with retention: each branch and trunk section is braked and guided to the ground with ropes, with no impact.
  • Bucket-truck felling: ideal when the trunk is hollow or too dangerous to climb via rope.
  • Mechanical stump grinding: we grind the stump up to 30-40 cm below ground level without disturbing the garden.
  • Administrative procedures: advice on felling permits with the cantons of Geneva and Vaud.
