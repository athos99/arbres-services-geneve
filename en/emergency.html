Storms and weather hazards

The emergency situations we handle

Storm, snow, black ice or hail weaken trees within hours. Here are the most critical cases where our immediate intervention makes the difference.

🌪️ Uprooted or overturned tree

  • Uprooted roots, destabilised ground, immediate danger
  • Felling with retention if the tree threatens a house or a road
  • Backfilling the hole and complete removal of stumps and branchwood

🌳 Split or decayed trunk

  • Longitudinal crack after severe impact or internal decay
  • Risk of sudden breakage at height without warning
  • Safety pruning, emergency cabling or directed felling

🪵 Hanging broken branch

  • Main limb fractured and stuck in the canopy
  • Unpredictable fall, danger to pedestrians and vehicles
  • Rapid clearance by rope or crane, without damaging the tree
Dedicated equipment

Our emergency intervention means

To intervene effectively, even in the most difficult conditions, our team is equipped with vehicles and specialised tools: snow removal, off-road, lifting and rescue.

❄️

Snow removal & access

Snow blade vehicle to clear access after a storm or heavy falls. Intervention on roads, car parks and blocked access paths.

🚙

4x4 off-road vehicle

Intervention on uneven, muddy or sloping terrain. Access to hard-to-reach areas where regular trucks cannot pass.

🪵

High-capacity trailer

Transport of large volumes: logs, branchwood, stumps. Complete site clearance in a single trip, without leaving waste.

🪢

Portable winches

Lifting and pulling heavy loads (trunks, stumps, vehicles). Secure anchoring for directed felling and correcting leaning trees.

🏗️

Aerial work platform

Secure overhead work after storms: removal of dangerous branches, cabling, inspection of inaccessible crowns from the ground.

🚗

Rescue & clearance

Extracting stuck vehicles (snow, mud, debris) and transport on trailer.

🚙 Rapid deployment from our base

Preventing rather than suffering

Most tree emergencies are avoidable. Targeted maintenance, at the right time, drastically reduces the risk of breakage during storms and avoids costly emergency interventions.

🌿

Preventive sanitary pruning

Removal of dead wood, dying branches and poorly inserted main limbs before winter. Greatly reduces the risk of breakage under wind or snow weight.

⚓

Dynamic cabling

Flexible securing of weakened main limbs or included crotches. Stabilises the tree without condemning it, preserves its physiology and natural load-bearing.

🔍

Free annual diagnosis

Inspection to identify at-risk trees (decay, lean, root structure). Written report with prioritised action plan. Zero commitment, zero surprise.
