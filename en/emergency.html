Storms & severe weather

The emergency situations we handle

Storms, snow, frost and hail weaken trees within hours. Here are the most critical cases where our immediate intervention makes the difference.

🌪️ Uprooted or overturned tree

  • Uprooted roots, destabilised ground, immediate danger
  • Retention felling if the tree threatens a house or road
  • Backfilling the hole and complete removal of stumps and branchwood

🌳 Split or hollow trunk

  • Longitudinal crack following severe impact or internal decay
  • Risk of sudden breakage at height without warning
  • Safety pruning, emergency cabling or controlled felling

🪵 Hanging broken branch

  • Fractured main limb blocked in the canopy
  • Unpredictable fall, danger to pedestrians and vehicles
  • Rapid clearance by rope access or crane, without damaging the tree
Dedicated equipment

Our emergency intervention means

To intervene effectively, even in the most difficult conditions, our team is equipped with specialized vehicles and tools: snow removal, off-road, lifting and rescue.

❄️

Snow removal & access

Snow plow vehicle to clear access after a storm or heavy snowfall. Immediate intervention on roads, car parks and access paths blocked.

🚙

4x4 off-road vehicle

Intervention on uneven, muddy or sloping terrain. Access to hard-to-reach areas where regular trucks cannot pass.

🪵

High-capacity trailer

Transport of large volumes: logs, branchwood, stumps. Complete site evacuation in a single pass, without leaving debris.

🪢

Portable winches

Lifting and pulling heavy loads (trunks, stumps, vehicles). Secure anchoring for controlled felling and correcting leaning trees.

🏗️

Aerial work platform

Secure overhead work after storms: removal of dangerous branches, cabling, inspection of crowns inaccessible from the ground.

🚗

Vehicle recovery & clearance

Extracting stuck vehicles (snow, mud, debris) and transport on trailer.

Preventing rather than suffering damage

Most tree emergencies can be avoided. Targeted maintenance, at the right time, drastically reduces the risk of breakage during storms and avoids costly emergency interventions.

🌿

Preventive sanitary pruning

Removing dead wood, dying branches and poorly inserted branches before winter. Greatly reduces the risk of breakage under wind or snow weight.

⚓

Dynamic cabling

Flexible securing of weakened or included branches. Stabilises the tree without condemning it, preserves its physiology and natural growth.

🔍

Free annual diagnosis

Expert inspection to identify at-risk trees (decay, lean, root system). Written report with prioritised action plan. Zero commitment, zero surprise.
