Sincerity, Listening & Plant Conscience

Our Approach & Human Values

We believe that caring for trees starts with attentive listening to people and a deep respect for natural balances. Far from aggressive commercial approaches, we prioritise the right word and measured gesture.

« The tree precedes us and will survive us if we learn to understand it.»

At ASG Trees Service Geneva, we never consider a tree as a mere decorative element or a mass of wood to be sawn. For Bendik Hausesman and his team, every specimen - whether a century-old oak, a family apple tree or a young indigenous hedge - is a complex living being, endowed with remarkable biology and a fundamental ecological role in our Lake Geneva region.

Our approach is founded on sincerity. When we come to visit your garden or estate, we do not seek to sell you unnecessary or traumatic interventions for the tree. If a tree is healthy and presents no risk, we will simply tell you to let it live. If an intervention is needed for your safety or the health of the plant, we will explain every step to you with clarity and kindness.

🌱

1. Respect for Tree Biology

We ban brutal topping and severe destructive pruning that weakens the crown and creates entry points for pathogenic fungi. We apply the principles of reasoned pruning, respecting branch collars and the natural healing capabilities of the plant.

🛡

2. Unconditional Safety & Peace of Mind

Technical arboriculture at height demands absolute rigour. We constantly invest in personal protective equipment (PPE) meeting the strictest Swiss standards, maintain our ropes and bucket trucks meticulously, and carefully secure every perimeter on the ground to protect your family and belongings.

🖃

3. Ecological Valorisation & Short Supply Circuits

Nothing is wasted: branches from our pruning are ground on-site or transported to be turned into natural mulch (BRF) protective for flower beds and vegetable gardens in the region. Felled trunks are cut for local heating wood or workmanship wood for artisan creations.

🤕

4. Human Closeness & Transparency

Bendik Hausesman is your single point of contact, from the first diagnostic visit to the end-of-project signing. No intermediaries, no surprises on the bill, and warm and fluid communication in French, English or German depending on your preference.

Whether you have doubts about the stability of a branch or an exterior fitting-out project, contact us for a no-obligation visit.
