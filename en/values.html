Sincerity, Listening & Botanical Respect

Our Approach & Human Values

We believe that caring for trees begins by genuinely listening to people and understanding natural balances. Far from aggressive sales pitches, we prioritize honest advice and moderate, thoughtful action.

"Trees preceded us and will outlive us if we take time to understand them."

At ASG Arbres Services Genève, we never treat a tree as merely an inert obstacle or a mass of wood to chop down. For Bendik Häuserman and his team, each tree—whether a century-old oak, a family orchard pear tree, or a native hedge—is a complex living organism fulfilling a crucial ecological function in our Lake Geneva ecosystem.

Our business philosophy is centered on sincerity. When visiting your property, we never recommend unnecessary or harmful cuts just to bill more work. If a tree is healthy and poses no hazard, we advise leaving it undisturbed. When work is required, we explain each step clearly with full transparency.

🌱

1. Respect for Tree Biology

We strictly reject destructive topping or aggressive lopping that creates decay and compromises stability. We practice gentle crown management respecting branch collars and natural wound compartmentalization.

🛡️

2. Uncompromising Safety

High-elevation tree surgery requires absolute rigor. We continuously invest in certified Swiss-standard PPE, inspect all ropes and rigging gear daily, and secure ground perimeters to keep your family and property safe.

♻️

3. Circular Wood Recycling

Nothing is wasted: branches and foliage are chipped into rich organic mulch (ramial chipped wood) to protect local flowerbeds and vegetable gardens. Solid timber is cut for local firewood or artisanal woodwork.

🤝

4. Honest Human Relations

Bendik Häuserman is your direct contact from initial on-site evaluation to final rake-up. No brokers, no hidden fees, and pleasant communication in French, English, or German.
