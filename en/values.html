Sincerity, Listening & Plant Consciousness

Our Approach & Human Values

We believe that caring for trees begins with attentive listening to people and a deep respect for natural balances. Far from aggressive sales approaches, we prioritize the right word and the measured gesture.

"The tree precedes us and will outlive us if we learn to understand it."

At ASG Arbors Services Geneva, we never consider a tree as a mere decorative element or a mass of wood to be sold off. For Bendik Häuserman and his team, every specimen — whether a century-old oak, a family apple tree or a young native hedge — is a complex living being with remarkable biology and a fundamental ecological role in our Lake Geneva region.

Our approach is founded on sincerity. When we come to visit your garden or estate, we do not seek to sell you unnecessary or traumatic interventions for the tree. If a tree is healthy and presents no risk, we will simply tell you to let it live. If an intervention is required for your safety or the health of the vegetation, we will explain every step with clarity and kindness.

🌱

1. Respect for Tree Biology

We ban harsh pollarding and destructive severe pruning that weaken the crown and create entry points for pathogenic fungi. We apply the principles of reasoned pruning, respecting branch collars and the natural healing capacities of the plant.

🛡️

2. Unconditional Security & Serenity

Technical arboriculture at height demands absolute rigor. We constantly invest in personal protective equipment (PPE) to the strictest Swiss standards, maintain our ropes and cranes with meticulous care, and meticulously secure every groundsite perimeter to protect your family and your property.

♻️

3. Ecological Valorization & Short Circuits

Nothing is wasted: branches from our pruning are chipped on site or transported to be transformed into natural mulch (chipped wood chip) protective for beds and vegetable gardens in the region. Felled trunks are cut to serve as local firewood or work wood for artisan creations.

🤝

4. Human Proximity & Transparency

Bendik Häuserman is your single point of contact, from the first diagnostic visit through to the final sign-off of the project. No intermediaries, no surprises on the invoice, and warm, fluent communication in French, English or German depending on your preference.
