Founder & Operational Director

Bendik Häuserman

Certified landscape arborist • Based in Vernier (GE)

Born into a family of florists, he has always held a deep-rooted passion for botany and living trees. Growing up around plants, learning their seasonal life cycles, and mastering their care naturally shaped his professional career.

Having completed formal training as a landscape arborist, and having worked in the field for several years, he built rich practical experience in top-tier tree surgery and landscaping before founding ASG Arbres Services Genève.

Multilingual and deeply attentive to every client's needs, Bendik personally assesses site conditions to deliver the most appropriate, cost-effective, and tree-friendly solution.
