Founder & Operations Director

Bendik Häuserman

Certified arborist-landscaper • Based in Vernier (GE)

Born into a family of florists, he has always had a deep passion for the plant world. Working among plants, understanding their growth rhythms and learning to enhance them is part of his family and personal history.

A graduate of Lullier School and a seasoned professional for several years, he acquired rich field experience in excellence arboriculture and landscaping before founding his own company ASG Arbors Services Geneva.

Polglot and deeply attentive to every stakeholder, Bendik surveys your technical and botanical constraints on site to propose and execute with rigor the solution best suited to your needs and budget.

Bendik speaks fluently French, English and German

Trusted Partners

Who are our services for?

Tailored responses to the specific needs of each client category in Geneva and the canton of Vaud.

🏡

For Homeowners

House, villa and ornamental garden owners wishing to preserve the beauty of their living space and their family's safety.

  • Careful work: ornamental pruning, light thinning and gentle reduction.
  • Expert advice: mechanical and phytosanitary condition diagnosis for your trees.
  • Emergency intervention: branch fallen on roof or fence after a storm.
  • Free and transparent quotes: clear pricing with no hidden costs.
🏢

For Property Managers & Businesses

Managers of real estate portfolios, condominiums and tertiary campuses responsible for the safety of residents and the public.

  • Multi-year maintenance contract: pruning planning to manage budgets.
  • Risk prevention: monitoring of overhanging branches on parking lots and walkways.
  • Priority emergency interventions: immediate responsiveness in case of storm.
  • Detailed intervention reports: traceability for insurance and condominium meetings.
🌿

For Landscape Professionals

Landscaping companies searching for a qualified partner for delicate high-altitude or complex-access operations.

  • Specialized subcontracting: climbing remarkable trees and crane operation.
  • Shared projects: synergy of skills on large-scale projects.
  • Evacuation & heavy equipment: large-capacity chippers and 4-tonne transport trucks.
  • Confidentiality and ethics: absolute respect for your client portfolio.
