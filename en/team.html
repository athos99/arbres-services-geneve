Founder & Operations Director

Bendik Häuserman

Qualified arborist-landscaper •-Based in Vernier (GE)

Born into a family of florists, he has always had a burning passion for the plant world. Working among plants, understanding their growth rhythm and learning to enhance them is part of his family and personal history.

A graduate of Lullier School and a seasoned professional with several years of experience, he has acquired rich field experience in excellence in arboriculture and landscaping before founding his own business ASG Trees Service Geneva.

A polyglot and deeply attentive to every interlocutor, Bendik studies your technical and botanical constraints on-site to propose and execute with rigour the most adapted solution to your needs and budget.

Bendik speaks fluently French, English and German

Who Are Our Services Aimed At?

Solutions tailored to the specific needs of each client category in Geneva and the canton of Vaud.

🏠

For Individuals

Homeowners, villa owners and ornamental garden owners wishing to preserve the beauty of their living environment and the safety of their families.

  • Meticulous work: beautification pruning, light thinning and gentle reduction.
  • Expert advice: diagnostic of mechanical and phytosanitary condition of your trees.
  • Emergency intervention: fallen branch on roof or fence after a thunderstorm.
  • Free and transparent quote: clear pricing with no hidden costs.
🏢

For Property Managers & Businesses

Managers of real-estate parks, condominiums and tertiary campuses responsible for the safety of residents and the public.

  • Multi-year maintenance contract: pruning planning to manage your budgets.
  • Risk prevention: control of branches overhanging parking lots and walkways.
  • Priority emergency interventions: immediate responsiveness in case of storm.
  • Detailed intervention reports: traceability for insurance and condominium assembly.
🌲

For Professional Landscapers

Landscaping companies seeking a qualified partner for delicate high-altitude or complex-access operations.

  • Specialised subcontracting: climbing remarkable trees and bucket-truck operations.
  • Shared job sites: synergy of skills on large-breach projects.
  • Removal & heavy equipment: high-capacity grinder and 4-tonne transport truck.
  • Confidentiality and ethics: absolute respect for your client portfolio.
⚠️
