The Founder & Team

A close-knit, qualified team driven by a sincere love for trees and nature. Discover who we are and the values that guide us every day on job sites.

Founder & Operations Director

Bendik Häusermann

Certified arborist-landscaper • Based in Vernier (GE)

Born into a family of florists, he has always had a deep passion for the plant world. Working among plants, understanding their growth rhythms and learning to enhance them is part of his family and personal history.

A graduate of Lullier School and a seasoned professional for several years, he acquired rich field experience in excellence arboriculture and landscaping before founding his own company Arbres Services Genève.

Polyglot and deeply attentive to every stakeholder, Bendik surveys your technical and botanical constraints on site to propose and execute with rigor the solution best suited to your needs and budget.

Bendik speaks fluently French, English and German

The Colleagues

At Arbres Services Genève, we prioritize networking and complementary skills. Around Bendik Häusermann, a team of professionals united by the same demanding standards of quality, safety and respect for vegetation. We work in close collaboration with Titouan Dechamboux, of La Compagnie des Arbres and Alexandre Dunand, of Branché Jardin, both qualified and specialized in their respective fields.

This complementarity of expertise allows us to offer precise interventions tailored to each tree and each project.
