Meet Bendik Häuserman & Team

A passion nurtured from childhood and certified expertise dedicated to Swiss trees.

Founder & Certified Arborist

Bendik Häuserman

Arboriculture and landscaping specialist • Vernier (GE)

Born into a family of florists, he has always possessed a genuine passion for the plant kingdom. Having trained as a landscape arborist and worked professionally for many years, he built solid field experience in arboriculture before establishing his own company ASG Arbres Services Genève.

Multilingual and deeply attentive to your needs, Bendik proposes and executes tailored tree solutions with precision.
