Humanity at the Heart of the Craft

Meet Bendik Häuserman & his team

A passion passed down since childhood and certified skills in service of the tree heritage of Romandy.

Founder & Passionate Arborist

Bendik Häuserman

Arboriculture & Flora Specialist • Vernier (GE)

Born into a family of florists, he has always had a passion for vegetation. A graduate of Lullier School, a professional for several years, he gained solid experience in arboriculture and landscaping before founding his own company ASG Arbres Services Genève.

Polyglot and deeply attentive to your needs, he proposes and executes the tailor-made solution suited to each tree and every situation.

Bendik speaks fluently French, English and German
