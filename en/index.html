Meet Bendik Häuserman & His Team

A passion passed down since childhood and certified expertise dedicated to the arboricultural heritage of the Romandy region.

Founder & Passionate Arborist

Bendik Häuserman

Arboriculture and plant specialist • Vernier (GE)

Born into a family of florists, he has always had a passion for plants. Having graduated from Lullier School and been a professional for several years, he has acquired solid experience in arboriculture and landscaping before founding his own company ASG Arbres Services Genève.

Multilingual and deeply attuned to your needs, he proposes and executes the tailor-made solution suited to each tree and each situation.

Bendik speaks fluent French, English and German
